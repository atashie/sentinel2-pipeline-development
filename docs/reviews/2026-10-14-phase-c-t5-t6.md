| Rule | What it fixes |
|---|---|
| P1 | Pairing. The Collection 1 item of the same datastrip, found through Earth Search's Level-1C item of the product's input name. Unpaired products are reported and left out |
| P2 | Grid. Every Collection 1 header shares the product's CRS and tile origin within 1e-6 pixel, or the scene leaves the comparison |
| P3 | Resampling. 20 m bands pixel for pixel. 10 m bands as the mean of four pixels, all holding data. B01 from the containing 60 m pixel |
| P4 | Units. Product values over 10,000. Collection 1 values times the declared scale plus the declared offset. Differences are ACOLITE minus Sen2Cor |
| P5 | Comparable pixels. Both values hold data |
| P6 | Strata. Three pixel classes at 20 m, and two quality subsets: all comparable pixels, and clear water by each option's own flags |
| P7 | Disagreement. A pixel disagrees beyond 0.005 reflectance. A band and class agrees when 90 percent of its pixels do not disagree |
| P8 | Spread of per-record medians, records with 100 or more pixels. Descriptive |
| P9 | The stored Collection 1 shortwave-infrared integers over water, against the declared offset. Diagnostic, never changes P4 |

## What T5 found

- Every one of the 59 scenes paired with exactly one Collection 1 item through the Level-1C datastrip, finding 34. The two split-datastrip scenes paired as finding 30 identified.
- No band agrees in any pixel class or quality subset under rule P7, finding 35.
- Over the 32.4 million interior observations both products call clear water, 25 to 81 percent differ by more than 0.005 per band.
- The 0.005 tolerance is absolute. In B11 and B12 the clear-water values themselves are 0.002 to 0.005.
- Lake Okeechobee holds 65 percent of those observations and dominates the pooled interior medians. Across the 78 records, the median difference is positive in B06 to B8A.
- The sign varies between lakes and between scenes. Lake Tahoe's blue record median is positive in 5 of 11 scenes.
- Clear water under rule P6 holds no negative ACOLITE value from 450 to 700 nm, since ACOLITE's own mask removes them. Negative shares therefore come from all comparable pixels.
- The stored Collection 1 integers over water are consistent with an offset of 1,000 still in place, diagnostic P9.
